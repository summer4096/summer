The owner confirmed the rights to include these fonts. Original binaries and
embedded attribution/license metadata are retained unmodified.

Compatibility exception: the app uses TengwarCursive-web.ttf, generated from
Tengwarc.ttf with FontTools. All glyphs were retained; the malformed legacy Mac
character-map subtable and VDMX table were omitted and font tables rebuilt for
browser compatibility. The original Tengwarc.ttf remains in the source assets.
